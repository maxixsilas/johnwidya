# Photos

Drop your pre-wedding photos here, then update `assets/js/config.js` → `media`.

Recommended before uploading (keeps the invitation fast on mobile data):

| Use            | Size (px)      | Format        | Target weight |
|----------------|----------------|---------------|---------------|
| Cover          | 1200 × 1800    | .webp or .jpg | under 250 KB  |
| Portraits      | 800 × 1000     | .webp or .jpg | under 150 KB  |
| Gallery        | 1000 × 1333    | .webp or .jpg | under 150 KB  |
| Closing        | 1200 × 1600    | .webp or .jpg | under 250 KB  |

Free resizing/compression: https://squoosh.app — choose WebP, quality 75.

The `placeholder-*.svg` files are stand-ins. Delete them once your own
photos are in place and referenced in config.js.
